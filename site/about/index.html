WHAT LINEUPBEAT DOES

Reporting first. Fantasy context second.

The goal is simple: make it easier to see the information that matters without blending verified reporting, projections and opinion into one thing.

01 · THE WIRE

Follow the people closest to the teams.

We follow local beat reporting across every NFL team, surface the fantasy relevant updates, and credit the original reporter and source.

02 · FANTASY DATA

Show the numbers behind the decision.

Projections, draft value, schedule, durability, coaching context and historical performance are built as separate tools so the underlying evidence remains visible.

03 · THE CONNECTION

Separate what changed from what we think it means.

A report can matter without automatically changing a projection. Evidence changes the view when it is strong enough, and the distinction stays visible.

HOW THE WIRE WORKS

Dozens of local feeds, one fantasy view.

NFL news rarely arrives in one clean place. It shows up in practice observations, press conferences, local reporting, injury updates and depth chart changes. The Wire is built to organize that reporting around the player it affects.

01

Track reporting from local NFL beat writers and established sources covering each team.

02

03

04

Credit the original reporter so users can see where the information came from and read the underlying reporting.

HOW WE THINK ABOUT FANTASY INFORMATION

Facts, forecasts and uncertainty should look different.

FACT

A report is a report.

If a beat reporter says a player missed practice or worked with the first team, that is reporting. Present it accurately and preserve the source.

FORECAST

A projection is our estimate.

Projected carries, targets, yards and fantasy points are model outputs. They are not facts, and they should not be presented as if they are.

UNCERTAINTY

Could happen is not will happen.

Injury concern, possible discipline, camp competition and uncertain roles should stay uncertain until stronger evidence changes the probability.

ACCOUNTABILITY

The stat line should explain the rank.

Rankings should come from the underlying projected opportunity and efficiency, not the other way around.

Forecasts will be wrong.
Facts should not be.
SOURCES & TRANSPARENCY

Show where the information comes from.

LineupBeat is most useful when users can distinguish original reporting, measured data and our own projections.

Reporting

Wire items should preserve attribution to the original reporter or publication rather than making the reporting look like it originated with LineupBeat.

  • Original reporter credited
  • Publication or source shown
  • Player or team connection visible
  • Reporting kept separate from projection opinion

Fantasy data

Data tools should explain what they measure, use published sources where applicable, and expose enough of the underlying numbers that users can understand the result.

  • Raw stat lines behind projections
  • Tool specific methodology
  • Historical data kept separate from forecasts
  • Updates handled on the appropriate cadence
